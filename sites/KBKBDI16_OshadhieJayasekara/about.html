About SnapMates

SnapMates is a creative photography group formed by passionate students who love capturing moments. We explore nature, people, city life, and special memories through our lenses.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to inspire creativity, improve photography skills, and build confidence in visual storytelling. We believe photography is not just about taking pictures — it’s about capturing emotions, memories, and perspectives.

📸 What We Do

  • Nature Photography
  • Portrait Photography
  • Street Photography
  • Event Coverage
  • Host monthly photo challenges
  • Share editing tips and camera techniques

👥 Our Members

SnapMates includes beginners, hobby photographers and advanced members.

Everyone is welcome to join, learn, and grow together.
